# U2c: the last small fixes

Six fixes from the UX panel, each test-first and committed on its own.

## The ready answer names the zone

- `_relay/ready` carries a top-level `timezone`: relay.edn's `:timezone`
  as an IANA region id. An alias reads as the zone it is, UTC in any
  spelling is `UTC`, and a whole-hour offset becomes its `Etc/GMT` zone.
  An offset no Etc zone has stays an offset. A property checks that the
  name keeps the offsets of the zone it came from. The changed
  notification does not repeat it. `docs/client-protocol.md` says so.
  relay-tui already reads the field.

## The main role is named, not steward

- The refusal of a leftover `:brief` names the main role (the
  dead-letter handler, else `steward`) in its `:daily-note` example.
- `relay note` says the main role writes the note's parts and gives its
  `relay routine run` line for that role.

## Note actions quote the item

- Acknowledgements and refusals for accept, dismiss, decline and revert
  quote the item through the same plain rendering the note uses
  (`digest/quoted`): refs as words, one line, cut at 120 characters.
  None of them shows a `[[kind:id]]` ref.

## Replies

- A reply is split at commas and semicolons as well as at newlines, so
  one line can ask several things ("yes 1, drop 2; revert 3").
- "yes to N" and "no to N" read as "yes N" and "not N". Only yes, ok and
  no take "to", so "revert to 3" asks nothing.
- Numbers alone after an act in the same line follow it. A number with
  no act before it asks nothing ("Thanks, 2 is fine").
- `relay revert N` on a suggestion says it is not a change before any
  "already done" message.

## One message for a request closed in a wake

- When a role closes an operator memo and also calls `tell_operator`
  in the same wake, the close's reply is held to the end of the wake (or
  of the conversation's turn). A `tell_operator` that names the memo
  stands in for it, wherever it sits in the wake, so the operator gets
  one message. Closes outside a wake are still told at once.
- The held state is in the log: `memo/closed` carries `reply-held`, and
  replaying the log gives the same messages.

## relay note

- Its lines wrap at 60 columns. A numbered or listed item's later lines
  sit under its text.
- Once today's note has gone out, `relay note` says when, and that
  `relay ready <id>` shows it. `relay note now` says the same after it
  sends.

## Checks

- `bb test` on the working copy before the commits: 1176 tests, 1176
  passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped.
- The status test joins its output lines with spaces, because status
  wraps at 60 columns and the old join split a phrase in two.
