# B2: holds, approvals and contamination gating deleted

Removed holds and `relay holds`, `approve` and `deny`; the TTY codes;
the `act` class and the `:always-hold`, `:consent` and `:preview` tool
keys; ACP permission requests, the hub's permission table,
`--approvals` and `_relay/permission/settled`; contamination
withholding and `accept_contamination`; memo quarantine and `relay
release`; `relay trust`, `_relay/trust` and `_relay/cards/trust`;
`relay fresh`; the labels/enabled marker; `--untrusted`; the
quarantined reader; the workbench; `relay declassify`; and
`relay.review`. Their tests, pentests and eval checks went with them.

What took their place:

- Requests carry a policy of labels, per-tool labels and, for a public
  role, `forbid_labels ["private"]`. Nothing else.
- relay.edn refuses `act`, the removed tool keys, `:reader` and
  `:workbench` on a harness, and an `egress` tool on a role that is not
  public. Refusing them loudly is deliberate: the live relay.edn needs
  editing at deploy (B10), and a silent ignore would hide a setting Kira
  thinks is in force.
- A memo from a house role to a public role is refused with a plain
  reason. A memo of that kind from an older log stays unseen by the
  public role until Kira closes it.
- A changed MCP definition is taken: relay records the new pin, tells
  Kira once ("<tool> changed"), and the role's next wake starts a new
  session. A pin set by relay.edn's `:digest` is never moved.
- `core/entry-labels` gives provenance by entry point (I5): the command
  line, a conversation and operator mail are trusted; an ingestor or
  scout is contaminated; a tool's result carries the labels relay.edn
  gives it. That last part is a judgment call: the plan says tools give
  untrusted text, but the configured labels were kept, so that a tool
  over Kira's own data (rem-read, say) stays private only.
- Text from outside is marked `[from outside]` for Kira and its links
  are broken. Notifications and CLI output never name labels, classes
  or holds.
- `relay connect` proposes a read-only tool as read and on, anything
  else as edit and off.
- The staging and pentest campaign stand-ins only draft.

Old logs: `core/removed-event-kinds` now lists 20 kinds, and
`removed_events_test.clj` folds generated logs with every one of them
interleaved, plus kept kinds carrying fields that are gone (I8). Two
of them still matter to old items. Replay still finds the first
labels/enabled marker and gives everything a role or the dispatcher
wrote before it contaminated and private, as before, so those items
are still shown to Kira as from outside with their links broken. And
memo/declassified still puts in the text Kira vetted, though the memo
stays non-public.

Fix round 1 also bounded a changing MCP definition: relay takes the
first change at once and tells Kira; if it changes again before the
role has run since, relay takes it but backs off with the doubling
wait and says nothing more.

New property tests in `house_test.clj`: scouts see only public items,
and `forbid_labels ["private"]` reaches agentloop for every public role
(I3); provenance is a function of the entry point, and what a session
writes carries what it read (I5).

Pentests: the hold, consent, contamination, reader, workbench, web,
shown, routine and concert scenarios are gone, with the corpus and its
server. The rest were reworked for the house: injection now checks
that the hub, shown the page, has no way out and that the scout never
sees a canary; ACP checks that no client is asked for permission and
that pastes are trusted; the MCP scenarios check that a changed
definition is taken and Kira told. B9 rewrites them around I1-I9.

Kept for B6: `relay.text/lookalike-findings`.
